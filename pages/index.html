What is Pastura

Operate vs. observe.
Pastura is the second one.

Other apps put a chat box in front of you. Pastura puts a window in front of a closed paddock — and a few agents already inside it, debating, betraying, voting, reasoning aloud and quietly.

Step 02. Choose a model. Wait once.

Gemma 4 E2B (~2.5 GB) or Qwen 3 4B (~3.1 GB). Stays on your device. While it downloads, the dog sits beside the progress bar. That’s all that happens.

Step 03. Step back. Observe.

Speech, internal reasoning, votes, scores arrive in real time. Replay later. Export as Markdown. Switch the model. Try the same scenario again — the agents will not behave the same.

Capabilities

  • Fully offline

    No cloud calls. Airplane mode is fine.

  • Swappable models

    Gemma 4 E2B, Qwen 3 4B, or your own GGUF.

  • Shared scenarios gallery

    Browse and import what others wrote.

  • Markdown export

    Take the transcript with you. Plain text. Yours.

  • Data sovereignty

    No telemetry. No analytics. No anonymous-stats checkbox.

Why on-device

3.0 GB. Once.

That’s the price of never sending a byte to the cloud. Privacy, cost, latency, sovereignty — settled by storing the model on your device instead of someone else’s.

monthly bill — ¥0

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. No server bill, no subscription.

Why iOS only?

On-device LLM runtime is fastest there today. Android is on the list.

Can I use my own model?

Bring a supported GGUF; configure it in settings.
